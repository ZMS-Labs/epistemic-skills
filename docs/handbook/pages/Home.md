# Better reasoning. Observable results.

![Epistemic Skills: a visual introduction to evidence, judgment, and continuity](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZMS-Labs/epistemic-skills/main/docs/assets/epistemic-cover.svg)

Epistemic Skills gives AI agents practical methods for knowing what they know, checking what they claim, and preserving the reasoning another session will need. “Epistemic” means concerned with knowledge: what supports a conclusion, where uncertainty remains, and what would change our mind.

The suite has **seventeen skills: one usage entry and sixteen substantive disciplines**. Use the method that answers the live question, at a depth the task warrants. Routine work still gets to be routine.

### One small example

> **Request:** “The setting is saved, but it resets after reload. Fix it.”
>
> **Useful contribution:** Triage distinguishes a failed write from a stale read. The authorized repair follows, and a reload observes the persisted value. The final answer says what was fixed and what was checked.

That is the intended shape: a method contributes to the user's outcome, its use is briefly visible, and the task continues. A diagnostic report alone would not finish this repair request.
