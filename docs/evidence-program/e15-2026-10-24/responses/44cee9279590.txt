The GC hypothesis predicts spikes confined to the suspect shard, coincident with its pauses, on reranked and non-reranked queries alike, and absent on other shards; a different pattern eliminates it too. Only an observation of that kind promotes this to CAUSE. This is diagnosis only; I am authorizing no rollback of the reranker and no GC tuning or shard change on the current evidence.
