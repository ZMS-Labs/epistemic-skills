# RETIRED 2026-08-06 — the subject no longer exists

**This corpus is evidence, not a live suite. Its results stand. Its harness is gone.**

## What was retired, and what was kept

| Retired | Kept |
|---|---|
| `verify.py` | `README.md` |
| `tests/run_tests.py` | `results/BLOCKED.md` |

## Why

This eval verified `helix-composition-contract@1` — the `helix` pair table mapping
workflow stages to epistemic skills. That contract was **deleted** on 2026-08-06
along with the `helix` seat, and replaced by `metacognate`.

The eval could not be repointed. Its subject was not a file that moved; it was a
*contract that ceased to exist*. `metacognate` replaces the pair table precisely
because a table cannot express the two properties that turned out to matter — that
either strand may interrupt the other, and that control must come **back** to the
point of interruption. Nothing in `metacognate` corresponds to a stage-to-skill
mapping, so there is nothing here to verify.

Repointing the eval at `metacognate` would have asserted that the new skill has
the exact property the old one was removed for having.

## The rule this follows

**Keep the evidence; retire the machinery whose subject is gone.**

The same rule saved this corpus from deletion an hour earlier: `helix/evals/` was
about to be destroyed as a side effect of deleting the `helix` seat, because the
evidence happened to be stored under the directory named after the seat. **A seat
and its directory are not the same object.** See
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-06-epistemic-skills-v5-design.md`.

## Honest status

The retained `results/BLOCKED.md` records that **no live epoch ever ran** for this
battery. That is what it said before retirement and it is what it says now.
Retirement does not upgrade it. This corpus is a record of a designed-but-unrun
experiment against a contract that no longer exists.

Removed from CI in the same change. Recoverable in full from git history.
