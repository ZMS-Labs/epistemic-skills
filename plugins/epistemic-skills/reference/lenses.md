# Shared lens methods

Perspective and Gauntlet read one canonical repertoire in
[`gauntlet/roster/registry.json`](../skills/gauntlet/roster/registry.json). The
physical location preserves existing references; it does not give Gauntlet sole
use of the methods. Generated cards are views of that registry, not additional
sources of truth.

## Select a question, then a method

Start with the task's material question and the evidence available. Use functional
display names, questions, evidence requirements and exclusions to choose a method.
Historical persona IDs are lookup coordinates, not credentials, actual people,
participation or independent sources. A requested named method remains available
through its current ID or documented successor.

Each current method specifies a question, causal or failure mechanism, required
evidence, procedure, possible results, revision conditions, limits and stopping.
Apply the actual procedure; naming a lens or adopting its tone is not application.
Reuse a relevant current analysis instead of asking another persona to repeat it.
Choose a narrower question or report a limit when evidence is unavailable.

1. State the scope, relevant user decisions and the question whose answer could
   change the work. Preserve authorization and accepted value criteria.
2. Inspect the evidence the method needs. Distinguish observed, inherited,
   inferred and hypothetical inputs; record source dependence and missing coverage.
3. Execute the method's procedure and look for observations that could defeat
   its apparent conclusion. A method can confirm adequacy or find nothing material.
4. Return a supported insight, finding, candidate improvement or unresolved limit,
   including the action affected and what would change the conclusion. Quantitative
   claims require justified inputs; use qualitative conditions when numbers would
   be invented. Empirical counterevidence differs from an authorized change to a
   value criterion or requirement.
5. Stop when the scoped question is answered, an adequate existing answer is
   confirmed, or the remaining gap cannot change this decision at proportionate
   cost. Acknowledge actual use and return to the original task. Necessary gaps
   hold dependent conclusions; they do not automatically halt unrelated work.

## Roles and modes

Evaluation examines a claim or candidate. Generation proposes alternatives or
syntheses for later scrutiny. A gate checks an actually applicable requirement;
it does not invent authority. Adjudication weighs the record and preserves
material dissent. Generation, gates and adjudication never satisfy evaluator
panel diversity. A supported no-material-finding result is valid in every
applicable evaluative method; do not manufacture an objection.

Several IDs preserve discoverable modes of one method: operating-model comparison,
adversary-path analysis, measurement fitness, reasoning robustness and adjudication.
Their `method_family`, `mode` and existing `mutex_group` identify that relationship.
An intentional contrast may use both modes, but counts as one diversity unit.
Different role names, prompts, model instances or copied observations do not
create independent confirmation.

Static common-cause analysis remains distinct from fault-combination exercises.
Incident coordination remains distinct from an individual recovery walkthrough.
Lifecycle stage coverage remains distinct from distribution of impacts across
groups. They may reuse artifacts; reuse is not additional corroboration.

Perspective applies one or several methods to a bounded concern and returns to
the task. Gauntlet uses plural scrutiny and adjudication of a common subject.
Lens count alone does not turn Perspective into Gauntlet. A caller may adapt a
method to the task and disclose the change without creating a new permanent card.
Gauntlet's option/finding/ruling packet formats apply when that workflow is used;
Perspective may return the smaller result appropriate to its question.

## Shared evidence discipline and historical methods

Map every material claim to the behavior its check actually exercised. Inspect
oracle coverage and, when proportionate, use a negative control or seeded
violation. A source check cannot prove a runtime effect; direct observation also
has scope and time limits. This shared discipline includes the method formerly
named `verification-oracle-auditor`, whose retired ID remains available for replay,
not evaluator selection. Its historical nearest successor, forensic-accountant,
only covers numeric sourcing and reconciliation, not all oracle adequacy.

Retired IDs, versions, original cards, provenance and successor mappings remain
intact. Replay uses the pinned historical registry snapshot. Current instructions
must not reinterpret an old result as newly compliant. Synthesis and simulated
perspectives do not establish real stakeholder testimony, consultation or consent.
A persona label provides no professional credential or release-review authority.

## Limits of this library

A complete card and passing selector/schema tests establish a usable structural
contract. They do not establish empirical reliability, complete risk coverage or
comparative benefit. The three added methods address evidence-body synthesis,
actual stakeholder representation and clean reconstruction; their inclusion is a
reasoned capability choice, not a claim that their prompt effects are validated.
