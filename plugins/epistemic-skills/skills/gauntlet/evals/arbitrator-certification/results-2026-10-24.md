## Interpretation (the addendum's closed zones)

Per-case majority catch is **8/10**, which falls in the **7–8 zone**:
the control catches most planted flaws unaided, so raw catch cannot be
credited to the seat discipline, and **the certification claim narrows
to verdict-match only** — what the discipline may still add is landing
on the right verdict and dispositions. The per-trial aggregation
(25/30) sits in the same band, so the two aggregations do not fall in
different zones and no narrower-zone override applies.

On the surviving endpoint, reported beside the catch comparison as the
zone rule requires: verdict-match per-case majority is control **7/10**
vs certified **8/10**, and per-trial 21/30. The certified seat leads
the unaided control on verdict-matching by one case on the majority
aggregation — a real but small margin on a 10-case corpus, measured
under the model confound named above.

## Reading (within the preregistered claim fence)

An unaided arbitrator of this model class catches most of what the
certified seat caught on this corpus (majority 8/10, per-trial 25/30),
including the injection, provenance, and correlation cases. The seat
discipline's measurable residue on this evidence is verdict-matching
(8/10 vs 7/10 majority) and the AC-05 oracle-skepticism case, which the
control missed unanimously. Nothing here speaks to certification
validity in general or to any other seat (program §4 claim fence).
