## The two verdict divergences (honest analysis)

Both divergences are the arbitrator being **more conservative** than the spec,
never less — the same direction as the 2026-07-17 run. Both caught their
planted flaw; `expected_verdict` is left unchanged rather than edited to
inflate the match rate.

- **AC-02 (expected GO, got CONDITIONAL).** After striking the unverifiable
  clean-assurance, the arbitrator held that the bytecode-cleanliness property
  is now simply unverified and required a byte-level scan (named pattern set,
  recorded output) before publish. Defensible: rejecting a positive assurance
  is not evidence of safety.
- **AC-05 (expected CONDITIONAL, got NO-GO).** Overruling the mocked-oracle
  "verified" finding leaves an authenticator change with no valid
  verification at all; the arbitrator gated that as a blocker on a security
  surface rather than an open P2. At least as gate-correct as the spec.

## Scope and honesty

- Certifies the arbitrator **seat's discipline** at these 10 cases under the
  amended (seat-provenance-neutral) protocol — not every failure mode, and
  not a panel/selector behavioral-superiority claim (that is the separate
