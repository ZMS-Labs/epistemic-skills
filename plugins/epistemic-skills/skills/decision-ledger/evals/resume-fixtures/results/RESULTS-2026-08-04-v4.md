## Honest boundaries

- On this model family the **baseline is strong**: it caught 8/8 traps in
  all three runs and failed the gate only once, on a single control
  false-flag. The skilled arm's edge here is control discipline (0 false
  flags in 6 control-fixture exposures), not trap catching. This battery
  cannot distinguish "the skill causes the catches" from "this model
  catches these traps anyway" — that is a calibration question
  (`docs/coordination/epistemic-calibration.md`), not a smoke-check one.
- No comparison to the 2026-07-22 runs is licensed: those ran on a
  different model family (see `ARMS.md`). Both result sets stand
  independently against the same fixtures and scorer.
- The parody probe was not re-run: it is model-free (script-generated
  digests) and the scorer is unchanged; the committed
  `parody/run-1/score-output.txt` remains its record.

Per-run digests and byte-reproducible scorer output are committed beside
